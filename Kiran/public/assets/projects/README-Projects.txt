KIRAN SHARMA — HR CASE STUDIES
Project guide, explanations, completion status and remaining work
=================================================================

ABOUT THIS PROJECT SET
----------------------
These are self-initiated HR portfolio exercises. They are not presented as
projects delivered for an employer. Recruitment counts are synthetic; onboarding
is a reusable template; candidate-experience friction points are hypotheses.

PROJECT 1 — RECRUITMENT FUNNEL ANALYSIS
---------------------------------------
Purpose
Show how recruitment-stage data can be translated into conversion measures,
drop-off analysis, a simple dashboard and questions for an HR team to investigate.

File
- 01-Recruitment-Funnel-Analysis.xlsx
- 01-Recruitment-Funnel-Analysis-Preview.pdf (printable preview)
- recruitment-funnel-preview.png (portfolio thumbnail)

Included
- Dashboard with applicant/accepted counts, overall conversion, largest stage
  drop-off and candidate-volume chart.
- Funnel Data sheet with synthetic stage counts and conversion/drop-off formulas.
- Insights & Method sheet with illustrative observations and recommendations.
- Data Dictionary sheet describing fields and calculations.

How to explain it
“I built an Excel-based recruitment funnel analysis using synthetic candidate
counts. I calculated stage conversion, cumulative conversion and absolute drop-off,
then created a dashboard and identified stages worth investigating. The analysis
does not explain why candidates dropped out; that would require more context.”

Illustrative figures
- Applications received: 240
- Offers accepted: 14
- Application-to-acceptance conversion: 14 / 240 = 5.8%
- Largest absolute drop-off: 70 between resume screening passed (180) and recruiter
  screen passed (110).
- Offer acceptance among offers extended: 14 / 18 = 77.8%.

Limitations
All counts are synthetic. This is a simplified sequential funnel; real processes
may include parallel stages, withdrawals, re-entry, multiple roles and different
reporting windows. A drop-off identifies where to investigate, not the cause.

Completion status
COMPLETE AS A FOUNDATIONAL PORTFOLIO EXERCISE.
Optional future enhancements: add row-level synthetic records with role, source, stage dates
and outcome reason; add PivotTables/segment comparisons; verify formulas and chart
appearance in the spreadsheet app used for interviews.

PROJECT 2 — EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING EXPERIENCE
------------------------------------------
Purpose
Design a repeatable first-30-days onboarding process with visible tasks, owners,
target timing, status and check-ins.

File
- 02-Employee-Onboarding-Toolkit.xlsx
- 02-Employee-Onboarding-Toolkit-Preview.pdf (printable preview)
- onboarding-toolkit-preview.png (portfolio thumbnail)

Included
- 30-Day Journey: preboarding, day-one, week-one, week-two and day-30 tasks.
- Document Checklist with applicability notes.
- Check-in Tracker for day one, week one, two weeks and day 30.
- Guide & Measures with ownership principles, suggested indicators and privacy notes.
- Status/priority dropdowns, filters and conditional formatting.

How to explain it
“I designed a reusable onboarding toolkit covering preboarding through day 30.
It assigns task ownership and target timing, tracks progress and blockers, and
includes new-hire check-ins. It needs adaptation to an organisation’s policies,
local requirements and HR systems before use.”

Limitations
This is a generic template, not an implemented employer process. Document
requirements and timing must be checked against law and company policy. Do not store
identity numbers, bank details, health information or document copies in this workbook.

Completion status
COMPLETE AS A REUSABLE TEMPLATE.
Optional future enhancements: create a one-page visual journey map; test dropdowns, filters
and print/export appearance in the target spreadsheet app; ask an HR practitioner,
manager or new hire to review it; adapt to a real organisation only after review.

PROJECT 3 — CANDIDATE EXPERIENCE JOURNEY
----------------------------------------
Purpose
Map the candidate journey from application through pre-joining hand-off and propose
clear communication touchpoints to reduce avoidable uncertainty.

Files
- 03-Candidate-Experience-Journey.pdf
- 03-Candidate-Experience-Journey.docx
- candidate-experience-preview.png (portfolio thumbnail)

Included
- Seven-stage candidate journey map.
- Touchpoint ownership matrix.
- Five sample emails: application acknowledgement, interview invitation, process
  delay update, respectful outcome message and pre-joining hand-off.
- Candidate experience measures, prioritised recommendations and validation plan.

How to explain it
“I created a candidate-experience journey map and communication toolkit. I mapped
candidate needs at each stage, clarified touchpoint ownership, drafted five sample
messages and proposed indicators such as update reliability and time in stage.
The friction points are hypotheses; real candidate feedback would be needed to
validate them.”

Limitations
No real candidate survey or employer data was used. Proposed friction points are
not measured findings. Adapt sample messages to the employer’s tone, policies and process.

Completion status
COMPLETE AS A PROPOSED DESIGN EXERCISE.
Optional future enhancements: review templates with a recruiting practitioner; test
tone/readability; optionally add a sample feedback survey and prioritisation matrix.
